Three shows, three contributions

Each of these musicals changed something different about what Broadway could do. Select a show, then select the contribution that belongs to it.

The shows

    What each one changed

        The pattern across all three

        Each show took something Broadway treated as decoration and made it structural. Robbins made dance carry narrative; Fiddler proved a show about a specific ethnic community’s persecution could run longer than any comedy; and Hair replaced the musical vocabulary itself. That last one is why Hair matters to this module in particular — it is Broadway conceding that popular song now belonged to rock and soul.
