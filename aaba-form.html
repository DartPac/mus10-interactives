Label the form of “You Send Me”

The song is built from four eight-bar sections. Tap each block to cycle its label between A and B — three of them share a melody and one does not. Then check your answer.

A – A – B – A

Sections 1, 2 and 4 all carry the same melody; only section 3 is different. That third section is the bridge, sometimes called the middle eight.

The bridge exists to create a need. By moving away from the main tune — usually to a new key area — it makes the final return of A land as an arrival rather than as one more repetition. Listen for the moment the melody stops being familiar; that is where B begins.
