MUS 10.5 · Activity

Find the One

Two rhythm patterns, four beats each. Play one, then select the beat or beats the band emphasises. If you cannot use the audio, the bar above each number shows the same loudness pattern visually.

The green bar above each beat shows how loud that beat is in the pattern — taller means louder.

Why this one change matters so much

A backbeat on 2 and 4 rocks you back and forth; it is a call-and-answer feel. Putting the whole band on beat 1 instead creates a hard anchor at the top of every bar, and the other instruments then interlock in the spaces between. That is why a funk groove feels like it pulls forward, and it is why the band can stay on a single chord for minutes without the music going slack — the interest has moved from harmony into rhythm.
